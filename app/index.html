    How rarity works here

    No earn-rate percentages are published for this game anywhere, so this guide shows none — any percentage would be invented. Rarity order is grade first, then Gamerscore: Platinum, Gold, Silver, then Bronze by Gamerscore.

    Points scales

    Gamerscore uses the 180 / 90 / 30 / 10 scale — Platinum 180, Gold 90, Silver 30, Bronze 10 — totalling exactly 1,000G. PSN points use the 300 / 90 / 30 / 15 scale — totalling 1,350.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot). GTABase and PowerPyx both flag it; other lists report nothing as missable. The guide hedges it and tells you to clear the spots as you find them.
