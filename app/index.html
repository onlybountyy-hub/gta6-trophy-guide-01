Roadmap to the Platinum

Three steps, in order, built from the official descriptions: the story first (it cannot be missed, but it unlocks everything else), then the open-world sweep while the map is fresh, then the 100% checklist that finishes the job.

Step 1

Play the story — and save like you mean it

The 12 story trophies (Chapters 1–4, Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory and the final mission) are unmissable, so just play. There is no mission replay and no New Game+: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission. In Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen and take the balcony meditation — see the missable watch below. And leave cheats off: they disable trophies and saving.

Step 2

Sweep the open world, with your partner

Work the stranger strands (watch the Strangers & Freaks counter — not every strand counts toward it), the robbery set (bank vaults after Chapter 4's optional mission, Sneakthief once Chapter 3 hands you the Snap Gun, shops and armored trucks, and a 4-star escape with your partner for Hot Together), the activities (shooting ranges, races, stunts, drone races) and the collections (cards, WAiNK scans, vehicle discoveries, meditation spots). Most counters feed the 100% checklist too, so this step is never wasted time.

Step 3

Close out the 100%

Open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion, and work the checklist down: the remaining robberies and gang operations, Bret's Target Decks, the underwater discoveries and whatever is left of the big counters. When the last trophy lands, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — pops on its own. Published estimates put the whole run near 200 hours.

Missable watch

One trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot). One of its 18 spots is Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation, available only the morning after the mission Down to Earth — follow her from the kitchen and accept. The spot disappears when Chapter 1 ends, and whether the trophy strictly needs it is not yet confirmed; take the two minutes and be safe. Everything else on the list can be cleaned up after the story.
