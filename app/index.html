Roadmap to the Platinum

Seven stages, in the order we would play them. The whole list is offline and nothing online is required. One trophy needs care from the very start: Positive Mental Attitude — its first meditation spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and is reported to disappear after that chapter, so it is flagged in Stage 1, long before the endgame sweep.
